# V3 Phase 11 — adaptive behavior and cognition proxies

Phase 11 extends the mature Phase-10 ecosystem into an animal-like behavioral branch without assuming that every multicellular lineage becomes motile, neural, intelligent or social.

The branch inherits the actual Phase-10 trophic energy flux, oxygen stability, productivity, food-web structure, ecological recovery and established multicellular lineage roles. Producer and recycler lineages may remain sessile or decentralized indefinitely; the animal-like branch requires an established consumer lineage.

## Functional state

The model tracks chemoreception, mechanoreception, photoreception, spatial vision, proprioception and sensory integration; neural integration, centralization, conduction, plasticity and energetic cost; locomotor mobility, endurance and maneuverability; resource search, avoidance, pursuit, ambush and vigilance; associative learning, memory, reinforcement and exploration; aggregation, communication, cooperation, competition and social learning; and reduced prediction, working-memory, behavioral-flexibility and internal-model indices.

A pursuit strategy cannot appear without an established higher-consumer lineage. Social cognition also requires individual learning first.

## Nervous systems are not a fixed ladder

V3 treats diffuse sensory-neural organization and centralization as separate continuously varying properties. It does not hard-code a single historical transition from nerve net to brain. Comparative biology leaves important questions about early nervous-system origins and centralization unresolved.

Neural information processing is also constrained by a separate metabolic-cost proxy. Increasing integration, centralization, conduction and plasticity therefore require sufficient trophic, oxygen and productivity support.

## Learning

Centralized bilaterian-style brains are not treated as a prerequisite for all associative learning. Experimental work has demonstrated associative learning in the box jellyfish *Tripedalia cystophora*, which has a compact rhopalial nervous system.

## Cognition boundary

`internalModelIndex` means only that a simulated lineage increasingly combines retained information, prediction and flexible action selection. It is **not** a consciousness, sentience, self-awareness or human-intelligence meter.

Likewise, `socialLearning` is not yet cumulative culture. Phase 11 stops before symbolic language, institutional teaching, technology and cumulative material culture.

## Rendering

The surface renderer places deterministic mobile populations on the exact selected world. Movement responds to locomotor state, while a second population layer becomes more spatially coherent as aggregation and communication rise. Rendered points are representative organisms, not a literal census.

## Evidence classes

- **A — experimental:** neural signaling is metabolically costly; non-bilaterian animals can show associative learning.
- **B — comparative:** living animal nervous systems span diffuse through strongly centralized architectures; sensory integration and motor coordination are major neural functions.
- **C — reduced/theoretical:** V3's exact neural threshold, cost/support mapping, lineage biases, learning/sociality equations and stage boundaries.
- **D — unresolved:** the exact number of nervous-system origins, ancestral neural architecture, and the evolutionary origins of subjective experience.
